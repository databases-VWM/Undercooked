# UNDERCOOKED
An undercooked multiplayer, menu-based, rougelite about restaurant management, combining elements from other restaurant management games with turned-based RPG and rougelite games.

## Gameplay
Parties of 2-4 players are split into two separate but intertwined gameplay areas: the **restaurant** and the **wilderness**. Resources gathered from the wildness by hunting go into a shared inventory that cooks in the restaurant can use, while money gained from selling food in the restaurant can be used by both cooks and hunters.

### The Wilderness
Players must head into The Wilderness to obtain Ingredients. Ingredients are passed over to the Restaurant via the shared Inventory. However, dangers can await in The Wilderness.

### The Restaurant
Customers come into the restaurant and ask for orders from the database of unlocked Recipes. Players in the restaurant must use Ingredients gathered by the players in The Wilderness and Equipment they have unlocked to serve the customer their desired meal in time. The quality and timeliness of the order can increase/decrease reputation.

The game is lost when the restaurant either runs out of money or its reputation reaches zero.
